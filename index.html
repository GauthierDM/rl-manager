How the season works (click to read)

Vocabulary

Open: a regional online tournament with 16 teams from one region. Each region plays 3 Opens per split.

Split: half of the season. Split 1 = Opens 1 to 3, split 2 = Opens 4 to 6.

Major: an international LAN event. There are 2 per season, one at the end of each split.

Worlds: the World Championship, the final LAN of the season, with the best teams of the year.

Split points: points won in the Opens of one split only. They decide who goes to that split's Major.

Total: all points of the season, = points from the 6 Opens + points from the 2 Majors. The total decides who qualifies for the Worlds.

Top N: the furthest round a team reached. Top 1 is the winner, Top 2 the finalist, Top 4 the semi-finals, Top 8 the quarter-finals, and so on.

Major and Worlds format

16 teams are split into 4 groups of 4. Each group plays 3 rounds of Bo3. The group winner goes to the Upper bracket, 2nd goes to the Lower bracket, 3rd and 4th are eliminated.

The Worlds add a play-in of 8 teams (4 reach the groups). The 12 best qualifiers go straight to the groups.

The LCQ is a regional knockout for the teams that did not qualify. Each LCQ winner goes to the Worlds play-in.

Watching the season

Click "Watch the season". Each Open of your region, each Major you qualified for, and the Worlds are shown on their own page. Use Next and Previous to move between them.
